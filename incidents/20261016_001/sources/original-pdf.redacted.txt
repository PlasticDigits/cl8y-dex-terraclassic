What we found

The transactions were signed with our keys but not by our bot, and not from any machine we run. We are treating
the wallet seed as compromised.

         Not the bot. None of the ten appear in the bot's own action log, and the bot's code has no bridge call at all.
         Not our trading server. No SSH login reached it during the 04:19–05:01 UTC window. Only routine scheduled
         jobs (monitoring and alerts) ran, and none of them signs transactions. No unknown process or recently
         changed script was found.

       Not our monitoring server. It holds no wallet key, and had no logins in the window.
       Operator's PC: no sign of use so far. When checked after the incident, no bot or unknown process was
       running and no suspicious startup entries were present. A full scan is still to do.
       Pattern: illiquid positions (LP shares, USTR, UST1, cUSTC, cLUNC) were converted into CL8Y and LUNC inside
       the wallets, while the stablecoins were bridged out first. The CL8Y was then bridged out too, in the second
       wave.

Open question: where the seed was exposed. We are auditing every place it has been stored.


Reported actions

Done (15 October, from 06:35 BST)

       Stopped and disabled all trading bots on our server, so they cannot restart on reboot.
       Paused every scheduled job that touches the wallets, and the automated review agents.
       Traced all transactions from both wallets since 14 October 16:00 UTC (this report).
